About

I am a computer vision engineer and researcher interested in building intelligent systems that have direct real-world impact. My work focuses on the intersection of computer vision, multimodal machine learning, and applied AI, with applications in healthcare technology and autonomous systems.

Currently, I work as a Senior Research Scientist at Frontera, where I build production computer vision and multimodal AI systems — object detection fine-tuning, video understanding for behavior recognition, document intelligence for clinical assessments, and speaker verification. Previously, I developed AI-powered assessment tools for children with autism spectrum disorder at CogniAble, and worked on computer vision applications in industrial automation at L&T Technology Services.

My research interests include computer vision, multimodal learning, action recognition, and machine learning for healthcare, alongside a continuing interest in robotics and deep reinforcement learning. I am particularly motivated by applications that can help people in meaningful ways — from assisting children with developmental challenges to making autonomous systems safer and more useful.
